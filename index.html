Introduction

Long ago, in a small village called Tadoy, Bolaang Mongondow, there lived a simple, friendly, and helpful community. The village was surrounded by lush trees, hills, and beautiful natural scenery. The villagers made their living from farming and always valued togetherness.

It was said that near the forest not far from the village, there stood a large stone believed to be the guardian of the village.

2
Chapter I • The drought

The Dry Season

Long ago, Tadoy Village experienced a very long dry season. The river began to dry up, crops withered, and the villagers struggled to find water for their daily needs.

A young man named Togop felt sad seeing his village in such a condition. He decided to go into the forest to search for a source of water.

3
Chapter II • A mother's advice

Into the Forest

Before leaving, his mother reminded him not to harm nature and to always respect whatever he encountered in the forest.

Togop listened carefully. He walked beneath the tall trees, searching patiently, while keeping his mother's words close to his heart.

4
Chapter III • The discovery

A Hidden Spring

Deep in the forest, Togop found a clear source of water flowing near a large stone. He was amazed and grateful. He hurried back to the village to tell everyone what he had discovered.

The villagers were filled with hope. Together, they planned to bring the water safely to their homes and gardens.

5
Chapter IV • Working together

Water for Everyone

The people of Tadoy then worked together to build a simple channel to carry the water into their village. The gardens became fertile again, the plants grew green, and life slowly returned to normal.

Everyone shared the work. Their cooperation made the village stronger and brought joy back to the community.

6
Chapter V • The guardian's message

A Voice in the Night

However, the mysterious voice was heard once more that night.

“Protect this source of water. Do not be greedy, do not destroy the forest, and never forget the importance of unity.”

From that day on, the people of Tadoy always protected the forest, cared for the water source, and helped one another. The large stone became a symbol of unity and a reminder that nature must be protected.

7
Conclusion

A Legacy of Unity

As time passed, the story of the Guardian Stone of Tadoy Village was passed down from generation to generation. The villagers believed that their village would continue to prosper as long as they lived in harmony and protected nature.

This story teaches us that a village's greatest treasure is not only its land and crops, but also its unity, compassion, and gratitude to God.

May we always care for nature and each other.
